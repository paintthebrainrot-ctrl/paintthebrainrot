PAINTING GUIDE

Your Figure.
Your Masterpiece.

Every bag comes with a collector card inside — FIFA-style, with your character's stats and color hints. Use it as a guide or go full creative chaos. Here's what you'll need either way.

MUST HAVE
🎨
Acrylic Paint
Any brand works great on the figure surface. Cheap craft store acrylics are perfect — no need to spend big.
OPTIONAL
🪣
Primer Spray
Grey or white primer spray before painting. One light coat makes colors more vivid and helps them stick — totally optional but worth it.
ESSENTIAL
🖌️
Detail Brushes
Get a size 0 and size 00 brush for the tiny details. Eyes, expressions, accessories — the tiny stuff makes it pop.
GAME CHANGER
🧤
Rubber Gloves
Hold the figure without smudging. Let each layer dry fully before the next. Patience = clean results.
FINISH IT
✨
Matte Varnish
Seal your finished figure with a matte varnish spray. Protects the paint, kills the shine, makes it look pro.
01
Read the Card
Pull out the collector card inside. It tells you the character's vibe, colors, and stats. Use it or ignore it — you're the artist.
02
Prime It (optional)
Light coat of grey primer before painting. Makes colors pop and stick better — skip it if you're feeling spontaneous.
03
Paint Your Way
Each brainrot needs to be painted its own way. There are no rules. Go weird. Go detailed. Go chaotic.
04
Seal & Clip On
Matte varnish to lock in the paint. Clip it to your bag. Let everyone ask where you got it.
